ESP32-S3 Si4732 Stage 1B — Hardware Connections

ESP32-S3 GPIO 8  -> SDA (Si4732 I2C)
ESP32-S3 GPIO 9  -> SCL (Si4732 I2C)
ESP32-S3 GPIO 19 -> RST (Si4732)
ESP32-S3 GPIO 1  -> Rotary encoder A
ESP32-S3 GPIO 2  -> Rotary encoder B
ESP32-S3 GPIO 3  -> Rotary encoder push switch

Observed Si4732 I2C address: 0x11
Serial Monitor: 115200 baud
Web server: port 80; use the IP printed at startup.

Important:
- Confirm electrical levels and common ground before wiring.
- This package does not include WM8731, Si5351, TFT/OLED, or FT8 connections.
- No antenna was connected during the reported Stage 1B validation; RF reception is not yet validated.
